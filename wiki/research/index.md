# Research method

IKAI uses an evidence-led research process. A plausible concept is not a result; a demonstration is not validation; a written privacy intention is not a privacy audit.

- [Research agenda](../../docs/research-agenda.md)
- [Research protocol](../../docs/RESEARCH-PROTOCOL.md)

## Minimum research record

Every experiment records its question, hypothesis, date, status, source commit, hardware/software, data source, data collected, processing location, consent basis where relevant, procedure, success/failure criteria, observations, limitations, alternative explanations, privacy review, reproduction steps and deletion confirmation.

## Evidence progression

| Maturity | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Literature review | Existing published knowledge and gaps | That IKAI implements it |
| Concept | A coherent proposed direction | Feasibility |
| Specification | Testable requirements and design constraints | A working system |
| Prototype | An implementation exists in a defined setup | General reliability or human benefit |
| Validation | Defined tests passed under stated conditions | Universal safety or product-market fit |
| Release | A scoped capability is publicly available and supported | Claims beyond the documented scope |

## Negative results

Failed experiments, unsuitable hardware, performance limits and abandoned approaches should be recorded. They prevent repeated work and help distinguish a real gap from an overlooked existing solution.
