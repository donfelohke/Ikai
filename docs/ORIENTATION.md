# IKAI — Orientation

**Domain:** Reflective-compute research  
**Status:** RESEARCH / WORK IN PROGRESS — **not a released product**  
**Design metaphor:** Mirror, not judge.  
**Portfolio role:** Independent research practice; not a NAKEBUSEN service or GENJE campaign product.

## Purpose

Explore reflective, local-first computing ideas — mirrors for thought, perception and memory — through human-directed interfaces and inspectable system behaviour.

The working definition is a human-controlled reflective system that supports perception, deliberation and choice while making its own behaviour inspectable.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No face identification, beauty scoring, person ranking, diagnosis or behavioural scoring.
- No covert profiling, hidden surveillance or default cloud profile.
- Minimise data; keep processing local where practical; document storage, transmission, retention and deletion.
- Make system behaviour inspectable and preserve user agency.
- Do not claim sensor integration, validated outcomes, accuracy or product availability without implementation evidence.

## Audience

Researchers, the author and technical collaborators. IKAI is not marketed as a consumer mirror product.

## Portfolio relationship

NAKEBUSEN remains the only commercial interface. WARENA may provide relevant infrastructure research or technical components, but IKAI remains its own research domain. GENJE remains the cultural and creative practice.

See [Portfolio Boundary (2026-10-31)](PORTFOLIO-BOUNDARY-2026-10-31.md), [Research Protocol](RESEARCH-PROTOCOL.md), and [Privacy Model](privacy-model.md).

## Research documentation contract

Every experiment records its question, hypothesis, method, data classes, consent/opt-out, retention/deletion, risks, results and non-results, limitations, maturity label, reproducibility details and review date.
