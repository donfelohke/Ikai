MIRROR · REFLECTIVE COMPUTE · RESEARCH

Computation that reflects without judging.

IKAI investigates reflective computing: the meeting of optical reflection, sensing, displays, local computation and human-directed interaction in physical environments.

IKAI is a Turkana female name meaning “home.” The name informs the project's interest in belonging, continuity, privacy and a personally controlled environment. It is not a technical acronym.

Open researchLocal-firstHuman authorityMirror, not a judge
Research status — not a released product.

Concepts, research notes and proposed experiments are distinguished from implemented prototypes and validated capabilities. No commercial availability or validated outcomes are claimed here.
