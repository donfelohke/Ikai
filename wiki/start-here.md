# Start here

IKAI is a research programme investigating reflective computing and human-directed interaction in physical environments. This page explains how to navigate the repository without confusing a concept, research proposal, prototype or validated result.

## What IKAI is

IKAI is a Turkana female name meaning **home**. Its technical descriptor is **Reflective Compute**. The name's meaning and the technical description are distinct: “home” is not an acronym.

The guiding metaphor is **a mirror, not a judge**. IKAI explores systems that help people inspect, reflect and interact while preserving human agency. It must not identify, rank, diagnose, score or covertly profile people.

## What IKAI is not

- It is not a released consumer smart mirror.
- It is not a medical or dermatological diagnostic system.
- It is not a beauty-scoring or person-ranking system.
- It is not a covert surveillance system.
- It is not a cloud-dependent personal profile service.
- It is not a claim that all proposed hardware or software has been implemented.

## How the project fits together

| Domain | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| NAKEBUSEN | Human and commercial foundation; first practical beauty-service context |
| WARENA | Libre-first, local-first technical capability and infrastructure |
| IKAI | Reflective-computing research and participant-facing interaction |
| GENJE | Embodied creative practice, movement, music and choreography |
| FELOH Corpus | Authorship, cross-domain terminology, evidence and governance |

**NAKEBUSEN + WARENA → IKAI** describes an application-and-capability relationship. It does not transfer commercial authority away from NAKEBUSEN or collapse the domains into one product.

## Status labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PROPOSED | A design direction or hypothesis; no implementation implied |
| CONCEPT | A conceptual model awaiting research or specification |
| RESEARCH | A question, literature review or method under investigation |
| PROTOTYPE | An implementation exists, but is not a generally released product |
| LIVE | A capability is publicly available and verified for the stated scope |
| HISTORICAL | Preserved record of an earlier direction or state |
| SUPERSEDED | Replaced by a later decision; retained for provenance |
| UNKNOWN | Evidence is insufficient to classify confidently |

A document's existence is not proof that its proposal has been implemented.
