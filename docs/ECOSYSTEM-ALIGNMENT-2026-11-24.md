# IKAI — Ecosystem Alignment Update

**Date:** 2026-11-24 · **Domain:** MIRROR · **Status:** Research / work in progress unless a specific record proves a later maturity state.

## Identity and purpose

IKAI studies reflective computing, mirror systems, embodied interfaces and human-controlled relationships between perception, deliberation and computation. The guiding principle is **a mirror, not a judge**. Its purpose is to support reflection and agency, not classify, rank or govern people.

A working, user-supplied name note associates IKAI with a Turkana female name meaning “home.” Treat this as an attributed hypothesis pending verification of language variety, spelling, pronunciation, morphology and community usage; do not present it as an established etymology.

## Research architecture

Keep the system decomposed into optical input, sensing, computation, display output, timing/synchronisation, calibration, human feedback and evaluation. The ordinary-mirror + adjacent-display + physical-encoder + deterministic-local-kernel configuration is a baseline concept, not proof of an integrated product.

WARENA may provide systems engineering and infrastructure. IKAI retains ownership of reflective-computing research, participant-facing design, consent requirements and evaluation criteria. GENJE may contribute movement and creative practice as participant-authored context, not automated interpretation authority.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No face identification, biometric identity matching, person scoring or ranking.
- No diagnosis, clinical claims, covert recording or hidden profiling.
- No default cloud profile or undisclosed external data transfer.
- Minimise data; prefer local processing; make capture, retention, export and deletion visible and testable.
- Participant authority and informed consent govern any capture or sensing.
- Distinguish literature review, hypothesis, concept, prototype, validation and release.
- Do not claim a capability or result without evidence.
