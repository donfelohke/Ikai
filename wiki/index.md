# IKAI Knowledge Base

The knowledge base is the structured reference layer of IKAI. It is organised like a technical wiki: topic pages explain concepts, link to primary research records, distinguish evidence from hypotheses and point to related articles.

**Project status:** RESEARCH / WORK IN PROGRESS. This knowledge base does not imply a working mirror, validated sensor integration or released product.

## Wiki conventions

- Article pages are explanatory and cross-linked; they are not substitutes for primary research records.
- Dated research documents preserve original scope, date, assumptions and provenance.
- Evidence labels describe maturity, not quality or importance.
- Proposed architecture is not an implementation claim.
- External sources should be cited at the point of use and recorded in the source register.
- Negative results, uncertainty and abandoned directions belong in the record.
