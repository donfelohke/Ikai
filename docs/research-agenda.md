# IKAI Research Agenda

## Questions

1. How can a reflective interface help a participant inspect an interaction without judging or ranking them?
2. What forms of embodied interaction can be represented without reducing the person to a persistent profile?
3. Which local computation patterns allow transparent data flow and meaningful participant control?
4. How can movement and Laban-related practice be documented while preserving context, authorship, and uncertainty?
5. What is the minimum data needed for each proposed interaction?

## Experiment template

For each experiment, record:
- Question and hypothesis, if any.
- Status and date.
- Prototype commit and hardware/software environment.
- Participants or synthetic data used, with consent basis where relevant.
- Data collected and processing location.
- Procedure and success/failure criteria.
- Observations, limitations, and alternative explanations.
- Privacy and safety review.
- Reproduction steps and deletion confirmation.

## Evaluation boundary

Do not use the research to identify people, diagnose them, score or rank them, infer sensitive traits, or build covert profiles. Claims about effectiveness require a stated method and evidence; do not infer success from an appealing demonstration.

## Current status

This agenda defines candidate questions, not completed research findings. Each experiment remains `RESEARCH` until its evidence supports a more specific status.
