# IKAI — Foundations

## Research stance

- Hypotheses ≠ findings  
- Prototypes ≠ products  
- Local-first preference; participant control  

## Hard boundaries (non-negotiable)

IKAI must **not** be designed or described as:

- Face identification or biometric surveillance  
- Covert profiling or hidden scoring of beauty/worth  
- Medical or mental-health diagnosis  

## Naming

**IKAI Mirror** — reflective metaphor; “mirror, not judge.”
