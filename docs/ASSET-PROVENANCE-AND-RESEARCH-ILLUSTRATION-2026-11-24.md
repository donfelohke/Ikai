# Asset provenance and research illustration policy — 2026-11-24

IKAI is a research domain for reflexive computing, mirror systems, inspectable interfaces and embodied spatial interaction. It is not a salon service or a commercial checkout.

## Illustration versus evidence

A generated image, poster, mock-up or conceptual diagram may communicate a hypothesis or design direction. It does not establish:
- a functioning mirror-compute prototype;
- validated sensing or inference;
- tested human-computer interaction;
- consent to capture or process a person's image or movement;
- deployed infrastructure or measured performance.

Use explicit labels: CONCEPT, RESEARCH, PROTOTYPE, TESTED, DEPLOYED, HISTORICAL or UNKNOWN. Use TESTED or DEPLOYED only when supporting records exist.

## IKAI-specific safeguards

Prefer reflection-first, repairable, local-first and inspectable design. Human agency and explicit consent govern any camera, sensor, image or movement data. Do not infer identity, health, emotion, intent or other sensitive attributes from an image. Do not reuse customer or founder photographs in IKAI research without an appropriate documented basis.

This document is documentation only; it does not claim a new IKAI implementation or change to runtime behaviour.
