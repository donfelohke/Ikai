# IKAI reflective systems update — 2026-11-22

**Meaning:** IKAI is a Turkana female name meaning “home.” **Principle:** a mirror, not a judge. **Status:** RESEARCH / WORK IN PROGRESS.

## REFLECTION±

The research shorthand is **LIGHT IN → COMPUTE → LIGHT OUT**. Treat this as an open systems model, not a completed integrated device:

1. Optical input/acquisition: reflection, transmission, illumination, field of view and capture.
2. Local compute/control: timing, processing, policy, storage and explicit human authorisation.
3. Display output: luminance, contrast, colour, viewing geometry, refresh and thermal/power constraints.
4. Human/environment feedback: participant interpretation and deliberate interaction, not automated judgement.

The mirror is one embodiment of reflective computing, not the research boundary.

## Research and engineering priorities

- Compare existing mirror constructions, optical stacks, two-way mirror materials, displays, cameras and sensors before selecting components.
- Separate optical feasibility from camera/sensor performance, display quality, compute, real-time I/O, thermal/power, ergonomics and interaction.
- For each candidate, record measured transmission/reflection, visible distortion, colour shift, brightness loss, viewing angle, ambient-light dependence, privacy implications and safety constraints.
- Define bidirectional I/O paths with timestamping, synchronisation, latency/jitter measurement, buffering, back-pressure, loss handling, permissions and physical/user override.
- Reuse and credit existing standards, research, open-source software and hardware designs; do not recreate a solved component without an evidenced gap.

## Agency and privacy

No face identification, biometric identity matching, person scoring, diagnosis, covert capture or hidden profiling. Capture must be off by default unless a clearly scoped experiment requires it and explicit authorisation is obtained. Presence is not identity or permission. Keep processing local by default and make capture, retention, export and deletion understandable and testable.

## Maturity boundary

Literature review, hypothesis, concept, prototype, validation and release are separate states. A document, CAD drawing, source repository or fork is not a physical prototype. No integrated REFLECTION± system, functioning mirror or deployed portal is claimed by this update.
