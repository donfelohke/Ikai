**Guiding principle: A mirror, not a judge.**

## Place in BLACK EXCELLENCE

IKAI is a distinct research practice connected to the wider **BLACK EXCELLENCE — GRACE · GRIT · GLORY** philosophy. It is not itself a fourth pillar or a released commercial product. It explores the relationship between home/environment, reflection, perception, local computation and human-directed interaction.

## Two engineering goals

### 1. IKAI SOFTWARE

Specify a native, local-first reflective-computing environment that coordinates:
- optical acquisition and explicit device state;
- local processing, compute and application state;
- display output and illumination control;
- human-directed interaction and physical controls;
- permissions, privacy, data retention and deletion;
- observable timing, errors and recovery.

Start with test-video or controlled camera input and a measurable display path. Define APIs, state transitions, timing budgets, failure handling and reproducible test fixtures before selecting unnecessary dependencies.

### 2. IKAI HARDWARE — M1 PROTOTYPE

Validate the physical reflective display independently of software ambitions:
1. passive mirror utility and viewing conditions;
2. glass reflectivity/transmission and optical geometry;
3. display legibility through the reflective surface;
4. ambient illumination and contrast;
5. mechanical mounting, serviceability and thermal management;
6. electrical safety and power control;
7. local-compute integration;
8. optional participant-controlled sensing and explicit capture indicators.

The mirror must remain useful when the display and compute are off.

## REFLECTION±

**LIGHT IN → COMPUTE → LIGHT OUT**, with feedback from the person and environment into the next input. The ± represents reciprocal input/output and feedback; it does not imply a passive mirror performs computation or establish photonic computing.

V4L2, DMA-BUF, DRM/KMS, GStreamer and PipeWire are candidate components/interfaces, not completed integrations. OpenXR is conditional on a justified spatial/XR requirement. Report latency, frame timing, calibration, uncertainty, optical conditions and limitations.

## Non-negotiable participant protections

- No facial identification, beauty scoring/ranking, emotion inference or diagnosis.
- No covert recording or mandatory cloud dependency.
- Capture is transparent, optional, purpose-limited and revocable.
- Local processing and data minimisation by default.
- Physical reflection remains useful with computing switched off.

IKAI remains **RESEARCH / WORK IN PROGRESS**. Do not claim an integrated prototype, validated sensing pipeline or released product without evidence.
