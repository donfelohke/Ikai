# NAKEBUSEN and Beauty Tech

**Status: APPLICATION RESEARCH.** This page identifies a candidate application domain; it does not claim a deployed beauty-mirror product or validated commercial demand.

## Why beauty is a useful first context

Beauty and loc-care already involve mirrors, consultation, visual reference, practitioner expertise and a person's own assessment of the desired outcome. This makes a professional beauty environment a meaningful place to ask whether reflective computing can improve a specific workflow.

NAKEBUSEN provides a grounded human and operational context. WARENA may provide technical capability. IKAI investigates the reflective interface and interaction architecture.

## Candidate workflow

| Stage | Existing human activity | Research question |
|---|---|---|
| Consultation | Discuss desired style and outcome | Can references be reviewed together without disrupting conversation? |
| Observation | Practitioner and client inspect locs | Can visual documentation be consistent and participant-controlled? |
| Service | Retie, retouch or styling | Is contextual display useful during service? |
| Review | Examine completed work | Can comparison support discussion without automated judgement? |
| Aftercare | Explain care and maintenance | Can the client access a clear, user-controlled care record? |
| Continuity | Return for future service | Does a record improve continuity without collecting unnecessary data? |

## Candidate functions

Client-selected style references; practitioner-authored consultation notes; optional before/after images with explicit consent; user-controlled service and care history; aftercare information; side-by-side visual comparison under controlled lighting.

## Boundaries

The system must not score attractiveness, rank hairstyles or people, diagnose scalp or hair conditions, infer health, or replace professional judgement. Images are incomplete representations and can be affected by lighting, angle, camera settings and optical distortion.

No image should be retained by default merely because a camera exists. Clients must understand what is captured, why, where it is processed, who can access it, how long it is retained and how deletion works.

## Validation before commercialisation

Establish a baseline workflow, select one narrow problem, test a safe prototype, gather practitioner and client feedback, measure time and usability, and determine willingness to pay. Do not claim market size, return on investment or product-market fit without evidence.
