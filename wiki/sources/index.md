## External scholarship and technical sources

Scholarly citations and technical references are retained in dated research documents where claims are discussed. When adding or revising a citation, record the author/organisation, exact title, publication venue or standard, publication date/version, DOI or stable URL, access date where relevant, and the specific claim it supports.

## Evidence register

| Claim class | Evidence required | Current interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Optical feasibility | Documented glass specification and repeatable measurements | Research required |
| Display readability | Luminance/contrast tests under defined ambient conditions | Research required |
| Camera quality | Captured test data and stated optical configuration | Research required |
| Camera/display registration | Calibration method and measured error | Research required |
| Real-time performance | End-to-end latency, jitter, frame loss and sustained-load tests | Research required |
| Privacy behaviour | Inspected data flows, permission tests and deletion verification | Policy exists; implementation audit not claimed |
| Beauty-service value | Workflow baseline, user feedback and willingness-to-pay evidence | Not established |
| Embodied interaction | Method, performer control, latency and contextual limitations | Research required |

## Provenance rule

A wiki summary must link to its primary record. If a claim cannot be traced to a source or an explicitly labelled hypothesis, weaken or remove the claim.
