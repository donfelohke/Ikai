# IKAI Governance

## Governing principle

IKAI is a mirror, not a judge. Its purpose is to support reflection and exploration, not to classify, rank, diagnose, identify, or covertly monitor people.

## Prohibited design directions

- Face identification or biometric identity matching.
- Person scoring, ranking, or hidden profiling.
- Diagnostic or clinical claims without an appropriate independently governed basis.
- Covert recording or surveillance.
- Undisclosed transfer of participant data to external services.

## Research process

Every experiment should state its question, participants or data source, purpose, data collected, processing location, retention, access, risks, consent basis, validation method, and stop/deletion procedure before collection begins.

Use focused branches and pull requests for substantive changes. Keep research notes factual, preserve uncertainty, and link ecosystem-level decisions to the FELOH Corpus. Support distributed stewardship and succession.

## Status and evidence

Use `LIVE`, `PROTOTYPE`, `RESEARCH`, `CONCEPT`, `HISTORICAL`, `SUPERSEDED`, or `UNKNOWN`. A written privacy intention is not proof of privacy behaviour; verify data flows and deletion paths in the actual implementation.

This file records design constraints. It does not claim that a prototype or privacy audit has been completed.
