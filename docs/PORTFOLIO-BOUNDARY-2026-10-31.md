# IKAI Portfolio Boundary — 2026-10-31

**Status:** Research boundary and portfolio alignment  
**Project:** IKAI — Reflective Compute  
**Metaphor:** A mirror, not a judge.

IKAI remains an independent reflective-compute research project. It is not a NAKEBUSEN product for sale, a WARENA product, or a GENJE campaign feature.

The current portfolio proposition is **NAKEBUSEN — Beautiful Roots. Sovereign Systems.** with the supporting line **Craft, technology and culture rooted in African ingenuity.** GENJE ZA WARENA — The Fire of the Formidable — is the cultural and creative identity. WARENA remains the systems-engineering identity powering NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL.

IKAI's purpose remains to explore local, human-directed reflection through interfaces, movement and creative computation. Its design must support perception, deliberation and choice while making system behaviour inspectable.

## Non-negotiable boundary

- No person scoring, ranking, diagnosis or identity classification.
- No face identification, covert profiling or hidden surveillance.
- No default cloud profile or unreviewed third-party processing.
- Local-first processing where practical, minimum data, short retention and participant control.
- No claims of sensor integration, validated outcomes, product availability or accuracy without implementation evidence.

## Public communication

When IKAI appears in portfolio material, label it **RESEARCH** or **WORK IN PROGRESS**. Do not make it a visual or commercial promise in the GENJE ZA WARENA launch campaign. The human remains the author and decision-maker.
