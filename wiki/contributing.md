# Contributing to the knowledge base

Contributions should improve traceability, accuracy, usability or reproducibility.

## Before proposing a change

1. Read AGENTS.md, GOVERNANCE.md, CONTRIBUTING.md and SECURITY.md.
2. Search the existing knowledge base and dated records before creating a new article.
3. Identify whether the contribution is a correction, synthesis, new research question, experiment or implementation.
4. Preserve the source and date of historical records.

## Article structure

Where applicable, include a concise definition and status label; scope and explicit non-goals; existing work and sources; technical or methodological explanation; limitations and unresolved questions; links to related articles and primary records; date and provenance for time-sensitive claims.

## Writing rules

- Distinguish observation, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
- Do not present a proposal as a prototype or a prototype as validation.
- Cite primary sources close to the claim.
- Preserve negative results and uncertainty.
- Avoid unsupported market, performance, privacy or safety claims.
- Do not introduce prohibited identification, scoring, diagnosis or covert monitoring.
